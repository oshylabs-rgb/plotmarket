How the pilot works

  1. Register as a developer.At plotmarket.ng/register. Your company name and CAC number are required.
  2. Request the pilot.On your Plan page, name the estate, its state and its area. One pilot per company, for one estate.
  3. We approve it by hand.The 30 days start when we approve, not when you register.
  4. We set up your listings with you.Up to 20 active listings, live for buyers to find and contact you.

After 30 days

  • Nothing is deleted. Nothing is charged.
  • Your 3 earliest published listings stay live on Free Starter.
  • The others are paused: hidden from buyers and search, but kept with all their photos and details.
  • To bring them all back, Business is ₦35,000 for 30 days and up to 100 active listings. One payment through Paystack. It does not renew automatically.
  • Or choose which 3 stay live.

Good to know

  • Each listing shows the title document as the seller states it. Plotmarket does not verify title documents or identity, and tells buyers to confirm at the state land registry.
  • Plotmarket never takes or holds payment for a property. Buyers deal with you directly.
  • Pilots are approved by hand, and approval is not automatic.
